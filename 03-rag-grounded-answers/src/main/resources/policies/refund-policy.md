# Refund policy — acme

## RP-30D-NOT-RECEIVED | acme | 4
A delivered order that the customer reports as not received may be refunded in full within 30 days
of the order date. The order must show status DELIVERED and the customer must confirm the delivery
address. Refunds under this clause do not require the item to be returned.

## RP-30D-DAMAGED | acme | 4
A delivered order reported as damaged, faulty or not matching its description may be refunded in
full within 30 days of the order date. Photographic evidence is requested but not required for
orders below the low-value threshold. The item does not need to be returned for hygiene products.

## RP-CHANGE-OF-MIND | acme | 4
Change of mind is refundable in full within 14 days of the order date, provided the item is unopened
and resaleable. Return postage is paid by the customer. Change of mind does not apply to
personalised items or digital goods.

## RP-IN-TRANSIT | acme | 4
An order that has not been delivered is never refunded under any clause. The customer is offered a
delivery trace, and a refund may be considered only once the carrier confirms the parcel is lost.

## RP-OUT-OF-WINDOW | acme | 4
Past the window that applies to the relevant clause, a refund is declined. Staff explain which
window applied and offer a goodwill credit where the customer has no prior claims in twelve months.

## RP-DUPLICATE-CLAIM | acme | 4
A second claim on the same order is declined unless the first claim was withdrawn. Repeated claims
across different orders within 60 days are referred to the fraud team before any refund is issued.

## RP-ESCALATE-LEGAL | acme | 4
Any request that mentions legal action, a chargeback, a regulator or the press is escalated to a
specialist immediately and is never refunded automatically, regardless of amount.
